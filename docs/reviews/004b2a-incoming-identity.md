# Incoming payment identity, aggregate and persistence review

Branch codex/incoming-processing, base 9f9af8b. The owner instructed implementation on 2026-10-04. Owner authorized committing this reviewed slice and its fixes on 2026-10-04. Merge approval remains pending. [Specification](../specs/004b2-incoming-processing.md#implemented-004b2a).

## Result and diff guide

A trusted, valid incoming pacs.008 read from an owned receipt now becomes one durable IncomingPayment per receiving participant and exact EndToEndId. Every receipt keeps its own original protocol references. Conflicting contents are held, and the payment has its own ownership and due scheduling. No CBS call, reply, worker or endpoint exists yet.

- **Domain/Inbound.** IncomingPayment registers with a normalized participant, the exact EndToEndId and UTC time, and raises one IncomingPaymentRegistered event. It has no lifecycle methods.
- **Application/Inbound.**
  - IncomingPaymentIntake returns Created, Existing, Conflict or LostOwnership.
  - IncomingPaymentWork acquires and releases payment ownership.
  - InboundWork.HoldAsync holds a receipt.
  - IncomingPacs008.Freeze and HasSameContents make the request comparison structural through read-only, value-equal lists.
  - Abstractions/Inbound adds the incoming payment repository (lookup and add) and the payment work repository. The journal work repository gains owned lookup, hold and receipt attachment, because receipt ownership guards all receipt changes.
- **Infrastructure/Persistence.**
  - New IncomingPayments and AggregateIdentities mappings.
  - Typed composite foreign keys from Transactions, IncomingPayments and TransactionEvents to the shared identity.
  - An event-name/kind check, and the journal attachment columns and constraints.
  - DomainEventsInterceptor now also prepares identity rows.
  - InboundJournalInterceptor became InboundPersistenceInterceptor, with immutable, write-once and owned field rules for receipts and incoming payments. Receipt and payment ownership grants are separate sets.
  - IncomingPaymentJson stores versioned envelopes.
- **Infrastructure/Repositories/Inbound.** Incoming payment and payment-work repositories, plus owned lookup, hold and attachment on the journal work repository.
- **Infrastructure/Inbound.** IncomingPaymentRegistration, and a FreshScopeRetry helper that InboundReceiptRegistration now shares without changing its behavior. Scoped and singleton registrations only; no hosted worker.
- **Migration.** 20261004154416_IncomingPaymentIdentity, generated by EF CLI 10.0.12 with `dotnet ef migrations add IncomingPaymentIdentity --project src/IPS.Middleware.Infrastructure --context TransactionDbContext --output-dir Transactions/Migrations`. Line endings and BOMs were normalized to the existing migration convention; nothing else was edited. The first generation had a nullable computed kind column. It was discarded, the committed snapshot restored, and the migration regenerated after the model marked the kind required.

The specification draft proposed two nullable owner foreign keys on TransactionEvents. The owner's implementation plan replaced this with the shared AggregateIdentities table and typed foreign keys, which this review implements.

## Limits

The comparison covers the internal request the reader produces. Any field the reader does not map is outside the payment identity by construction. CBS's own scoping of EndToEndId is still unverified, as recorded in the specification. The migration supports fresh databases only.

SQL ties events and state to an identity of the matching kind, but only the interceptor guarantees that each identity also has a state row. Conflict-held receipts now retain their trusted original references independently of payment attachment; reply behavior remains deferred to its own capability review.
